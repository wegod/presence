## Blockers

1. **Host thread/process limit** (`ulimit -u=2666`): threads die at ~7500; xargs forks die at ~1500.
2. **Client/network saturation above ~5–6k**: async 10k completes but majority `http_0` (connect failures), not server 5xx business codes — Colima/Docker publish path is the bottleneck, not oversell logic.
3. **Sandbox note**: Cursor sandbox breaks `xargs` (`sysconf(_SC_ARG_MAX) failed`); run bench outside sandbox for xargs mode.
4. **Correctness**: across all completed rounds, **oversell=false**, success_count=100=STOCK.
